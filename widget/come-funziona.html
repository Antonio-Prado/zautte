How Zautte works

Zautte is a virtual assistant for an organisation's website. It is an artificial intelligence system: answers are written automatically by a program, not by a person. The service is experimental.

Where its answers come from

When you type a question, Zautte looks for the most relevant passages among the pages of the organisation's institutional website, the published documents and some information sheets prepared by the offices. It then passes those passages to a language model, chosen by the organisation, which writes the answer using only that information. Below each answer you will find the sources used, so you can check them.

What it does not do

  • It does not make decisions or start procedures: for applications, requests and certificates, the organisation's offices remain responsible.
  • Its answers have no official value. It may be wrong or give incomplete information: always check important information in the sources shown or with the relevant office.
  • It does not know your personal situation and does not access the organisation's databases or archives that contain personal data.

Your data

  • Do not enter personal data in the chat (names, tax codes, addresses, phone numbers, health conditions…).
  • Before any processing, Zautte automatically replaces tax codes, IBANs, card numbers, email addresses and phone numbers with a placeholder. Names and other freely written data cannot be reliably recognised.
  • The question, already masked, the latest messages of the conversation and the website passages are sent to the model provider only to generate the answer, under the conditions set by the organisation.
  • The organisation may keep your questions and Zautte's answers to improve the service: retention periods are stated in the personal data processing notice.
  • The comment you write after a 👎 is saved exactly as you write it, without masking: do not include personal data in it.

Human oversight

With the 👍 and 👎 buttons below each answer you can say whether it was helpful. The reports are read by the organisation's staff, who correct missing or wrong information. Responsibility for the service remains with the organisation.

Accessibility

The chat is designed to be usable with the keyboard alone and with screen readers, aiming for level AA of the WCAG 2.1 guidelines required of public administrations. Every change is checked automatically; testing with screen readers and with people with disabilities is still to be completed. The up-to-date status is on the project's accessibility page. If you run into difficulties, report them with 👎 and a comment below an answer: you help us improve it.

References: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on artificial intelligence (art. 50), Italian Law no. 132 of 23 September 2025 (art. 14).
